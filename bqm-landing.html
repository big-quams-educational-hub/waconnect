❓ FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about BQM before signing up.

Is BQM really completely free?
+
Yes — 100% free for all verified Nigerian students. BQM is a free educational platform. You will never be charged for any tool, book, or feature on the platform. No subscriptions, no hidden fees, no premium tiers.
Why does verification take 24–48 hours?
+
BQM is a student-only platform. Our admin team manually reviews every application to ensure that only real Nigerian students are verified. This protects the community and ensures the tools and content remain relevant. You'll receive an email notification once approved.
What documents do I need to verify my account?
+
You'll need to upload a valid Student ID, National ID, or JAMB slip — plus proof of enrolment such as an admission letter or school fee receipt. Secondary school students can upload their school ID and a parent/guardian letter. All documents are reviewed securely and never shared.
Can secondary school (SS) students use BQM?
+
Absolutely. BQM is built for both secondary school students preparing for WAEC, NECO, and JAMB — and university students managing their academic journey. The CBT practice, past questions, and eLibrary are especially useful for SS2 and SS3 students.
How is BQM different from other Nigerian education platforms?
+
BQM combines tools that are usually scattered across multiple apps: CBT practice, GPA tracking, scholarship finder, NELFUND guide, eLibrary, news feed, and a social community — all in one place, all free. We also keep content updated weekly and verify all students for a safer community.
How do I get help if I have a problem?
+
You can reach BQM support via WhatsApp at +234 904 987 1643, through the in-app help section, or via our community on WhatsApp. Our team typically responds within a few hours during working days.
